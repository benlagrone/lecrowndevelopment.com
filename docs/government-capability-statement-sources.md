# Government capability statement source notes

The downloadable one-page capability statement follows Marina Coryat's July 16, 2026 review and the attached UH APEX Accelerator materials:

- `Capability Statement Checklist-Final--02.19.25.xlsx`
- `Capabilities Statement Template.docx`

Applied requirements:

- one page, one side, distributed as a PDF under 1 MB;
- four primary sections: Core Competencies, Company Experience, Differentiators, and Company Data;
- no third-party company logos or client point-of-contact information;
- recent/relevant work may be identified as key-personnel experience;
- three to five objective, measurable, and verifiable differentiators;
- a limited set of commodity codes aligned to the stated services;
- no unverified CAGE, UEI, socioeconomic certification, clearance, or contract vehicle claims.
